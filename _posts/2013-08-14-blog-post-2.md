I recently had the opportunity to share my story with **SIM-UB**, the school where everything truly began for me.

When I joined the University at Buffalo program at SIM, I wasn’t the most conventional student. I struggled in junior college and retook my A-Levels—only to fall short again. I was offered programs that didn’t align with my passion until I found the **UB Economics** program. That decision changed the course of my life.

At **SIM-UB**, I rediscovered my love for learning. The American-style education emphasized participation and critical thinking. Professors like **Sandeep** and **Thomas** saw potential in me I hadn’t yet seen in myself—and encouraged me to aim higher, even planting the idea of pursuing a PhD.

I went from being a student who almost gave up on academics to one now completing a **Master’s in Economics at SMU**, with plans to pursue a doctorate next year. Along the way, I’ve worked on research in **behavioral economics**, reconnected with my cultural roots through an arts internship, and built lifelong friendships.

To any student wondering if private education in Singapore can hold its own—yes, it absolutely can. It’s not the name of the school that defines your path, but what you do with the opportunities you’re given. **SIM-UB** gave me the structure, support, and space to grow.

Thank you to the incredible faculty, mentors, and peers who were part of this journey. 💙
