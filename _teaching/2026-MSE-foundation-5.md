**Course:** ECON 6018 (Master’s Level)  
**Instructor:** Teresa Ng  
**Date:** 20 February 2026  

This tutorial focuses on **Implicit Differentiation** and extends it to **second-order derivatives** and **differentiation with respect to a third variable**.  
The goal is exam-ready fluency: recognizing patterns fast, differentiating cleanly, and avoiding common algebra traps.

---

## Topics Covered

* Quick recall: basic differentiation setup  
* Implicit differentiation (single equation, mixed terms)  
* Chain rule in implicit settings  
* Practice set: exam-style implicit differentiation questions  
* Second-order derivatives from implicit equations  
* Differentiation with respect to a third variable (e.g., \(z\))  
* Worked answers (step-by-step)

## Notes

This tutorial is especially useful for:

* Comparative statics (implicit function relationships)  
* Solving equilibrium conditions without explicit solutions  
* Hessian/curvature work when functions are defined implicitly  
* Handling multivariable differentiation in micro/econometrics

Students should aim to practice until the differentiation steps become automatic — speed matters a lot in exams.
