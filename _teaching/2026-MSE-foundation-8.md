**Course:** ECON 6018 (Master’s Level)  
**Instructor:** Teresa Ng  
**Date:** 27 March 2026  

This tutorial focuses on **Unconstrained Optimization of Multivariate Functions**, including the use of **first-order conditions (FOC)** and **second-order conditions (SOC)** to classify stationary points. It also applies these ideas to **utility functions**, **firm profit functions**, and **comparative statics**. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## Topics Covered

* Unconstrained Optimization
* Multivariate Functions
* First-Order Conditions (FOC)
* Second-Order Conditions (SOC)
* Utility Function Application
* Profit Function Application
* Comparative Statics in Profit Maximization
